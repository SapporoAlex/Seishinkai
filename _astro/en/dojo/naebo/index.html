Seishinkai Karate-do Federation — Honmachi Branch (formerly Naebo Branch)

[Honmachi Karate Class, Higashi Ward, Seishinkai Honmachi Branch]
This class is currently suspended.
The Naebo Karate Class started in October 2010 (Heisei 22) at the Naebo Children's Hall. From 2023 (Reiwa 5), the Naebo Children's Hall relocated inside Honmachi Elementary School and became the "Naebo-Honmachi Children's Hall," and the class relaunched as the Honmachi Karate Class.
A karate club centered on Kids and Junior classes.
We especially welcome participation from parents and children together, and from the Senior Class.
While many dojos feel like an extension of school sports clubs, we aim to make karate an enjoyable, club-like experience centered on health and fitness.

※Temporarily suspended since May 2024 (Reiwa 6). Active from 2010, activity at this venue paused at the end of April 2024. We would like to resume once around 5 or more members have gathered, so please get in touch if you're interested.
Since the venue is a rented facility, please direct inquiries to the office.
