Seishinkan Shiroishi Karate Dojo (Shiroishi Headquarters)

Karate Dojo in Shiroishi Ward

The Headquarters Dojo was established as a permanent training facility in March 1986 in Shiroishi Ward, Sapporo. Including its years as the former Sapporo Branch, it has served the community for over 50 years, making it one of Sapporo's longest-established karate dojos.

It served as the organization's Headquarters Dojo until the current Seishinkai Headquarters building on Sakae-dori in Shiroishi Ward was completed in 2012.

Since August 2026, training has been held on the 2nd Floor of Shiroishi Welfare Hall.

As one of the oldest karate dojos in Shiroishi Ward, instruction is provided directly under the supervision of Headquarters, with experienced black belt instructors rotating teaching duties.

Today, the dojo primarily offers junior classes for preschool, elementary, and junior high school students.

The venue is conveniently located between National Route 12 and Hongō-dōri.
