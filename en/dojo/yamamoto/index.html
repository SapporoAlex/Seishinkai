Seishinkan Yamamoto Records Storage

The Seishinkan Yamamoto Training Center was established in May 2014 (Heisei 26) by converting a warehouse into a space where meetings and training sessions could be held, and where visitors from regional branches or overseas could stay in simple lodging.
Originally it also had space set aside for practice, but the lodging function has since moved to the Hondori Seishinkan, and this location now serves as a storage facility for records and materials.

Former Seishinkan Yamamoto Training Center (Atsubetsu-cho Yamamoto, Atsubetsu-ku, Sapporo)

Seishinkan Yamamoto Training Facility

Site area: 200 tsubo / Floor area: 120 tsubo

Memorial Room of the Late Director Kayoko Mimura

Personal items from that time are kept here.

※This facility is used for meetings and storage, and no classes are held here. For training days and venues, please see the list of Karate Dojos & Classes in Atsubetsu Ward.
