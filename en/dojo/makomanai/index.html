Seishinkai Karate-do Federation – Makomanai Branch

Minami-ku Makomanai Karate Class – Seishinkai Makomanai Branch

The Makomanai Karate Class started in 2007 at the Makomanai Gorin Children's Center. In 2017, as membership declined, it merged with the Moiwa and Tokiwa branches, but it reopened in September 2019. Following the consolidation of local elementary schools, the Children's Center closed, and since August 2026 classes have been held in the meeting room of the Minami-ku Citizens' Center. We aim to focus on kids' and junior karate, welcoming participants from preschool age through junior high school. We are currently accepting trial participants!

We also tend to have more parent-and-child participation than other branches. While many dojos feel like an extension of a school sports club, we aim to make training feel more like a relaxed circle activity — enjoying karate as a way to build health and fitness while still learning its true spirit. *Depending on further school consolidations, we may move from the current Citizens' Center to a new children's center or another suitable venue if one becomes available.

Seishinkai Karate: cultivating a spirit that never gives up and never breaks
