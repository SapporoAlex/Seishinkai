Seishinkai Karate-Do Federation – Hondori Branch

Shiroishi Ward Hondori Karate Classes / Seishinkan Hondori Dojo

The Hondori karate class began in September 2011 at the Hondori Seishinkan Dojo, located along National Route 12. It sits next to Yakiniku Tokuju, and directly across from Round One, making it easy to find. Since its founding, the dojo has focused on kids' and junior karate, welcoming students from preschool age through junior high school. Both the Wednesday and Friday classes are capped at 15 students each, and the venue is fully air-conditioned. While many dojos feel like an extension of school sports clubs, we aim for a more relaxed, club-like atmosphere where students can enjoy learning karate for health and fitness.

Building an unyielding, unbreakable spirit — Seishinkai Karate
