Seishinkai Karate Federation — Momijidai Branch

[Momijidai Karate Class, Atsubetsu-ku / Seishinkai Momijidai Branch]
The Momijidai Karate Class began its activities at the Momijidai Management Center in April 2010 (Heisei 22).
Since its founding, it started as Kids Karate and Junior Karate, and now welcomes a wide range of participants, from preschoolers to adults (parent and child).
Parent-and-child participation is especially welcome.
While many dojos feel like an extension of school sports-club culture, we aim for karate as health and fitness — practiced and learned with the relaxed enjoyment of a community club.
