Seishinkai Karate Federation — Shizuoka Prefecture Branch

Hokureikai · Misawaji Temple Karate Dojo · Seishinkai Shizuoka Branch

Misawaji Temple Dojo was established by Jushin Inuura, who trained at the Seishinkai Headquarters Dojo and founded the Minobusan University Karate Club in Yamanashi Prefecture in 2001.
In June 2005, he established Misawaji Temple Dojo at Misawaji Temple in Shizuoka Prefecture.
Participants range from children to seniors.
