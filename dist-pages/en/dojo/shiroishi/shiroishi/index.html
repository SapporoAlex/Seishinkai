Seishinkan Shiroishi Karate Dojo (Shiroishi Headquarters)

Karate Dojo in Shiroishi Ward

The Headquarters Dojo was established as a permanent training facility in Shiroishi Ward in March 1986 and has remained active for more than 35 years.
Counting from the days of the former Sapporo Branch, karate training has continued here for over 40 years, making it one of Sapporo's longest-established karate dojos.
Until the federation's General Headquarters was established in 2012, this facility served as the main headquarters dojo. It is now known as the permanent Shiroishi Dojo.
From August 2026, training moved to the 2nd floor of the Shiroishi Welfare Hall.
It is considered one of the oldest karate dojos in Shiroishi Ward and is operated directly by Seishinkai, with instructors rotating on a regular basis.
Today, the dojo primarily offers junior classes for kindergarten and junior high school students.
The venue is conveniently located between National Route 12 and Hongō-dori.
