Seishinkai Karate Federation — Hondori Branch

[Hondori Karate Class, Shiroishi Ward / Seishinkan Hondori Dojo]
The Hondori Karate Class began activities in September 2011 (Heisei 23) at the Hondori Seishinkan dojo, facing National Route 12.
It's in an easy-to-find location — next to the "Tokuju" yakiniku restaurant, with Round1 just across the street.
Since its founding, it has run as a Kids Karate / Junior Karate class, focused mainly on preschoolers through junior high school students.
The Wednesday and Friday classes are each capped at 15 students, and the venue is fully air-conditioned.
While many dojos feel like an extension of school sports-club activities, we aim for karate as health and fitness training — practicing and learning together in a relaxed, club-like atmosphere.
