Seishinkai Karate Federation – Hondori Branch

[Hondori Karate Class – Seishinkan Hondori Dojo]
The Hondori Karate Class has been operating at Seishinkan Hondori Dojo, located along National Route 12, since September 2011.
The dojo is easy to find, located next to Tokuju Yakiniku Restaurant and across from Round One.
Since its establishment, the dojo has focused primarily on children's and junior karate classes, while also welcoming parents and children who wish to train together.
As this is a dedicated dojo with limited space, each class is limited to 15 students.
The dojo is fully air-conditioned.
Rather than the atmosphere of a traditional competitive sports club, we aim to provide a friendly environment where students can enjoy learning karate for health, fitness, and personal development.
