HEADQUARTERS

Seishinkan Okubo Headquarters Dojo

Located on Tohoku-dori Avenue, on the border between Shiroishi and Toyohira Wards in Sapporo

Our headquarters dojo has operated as a permanent training facility since March 1986.
Continuing the tradition established during the former Sapporo Branch era, it is one of Sapporo's longest-established karate dojos, with nearly half a century of history.
The dojo moved to its current location in 2012 and now features dedicated parking and approximately 660㎡ (about 7,100 sq ft) of training space across the main and annex buildings, making it one of the largest permanent karate facilities in Hokkaido.
Students range in age from 3 to 84 years old and enjoy training together in a friendly atmosphere.
Classes include Junior Classes (Mon/Wed), Adult Classes (Wed/Sat), Kindergarten & Elementary Classes (Thu), Women's Class (Fri), Parent & Child Class (Sat), Daytime Senior Classes (Mon/Wed), and an Iaido Club (Sun).
We also offer a newly established special class for students who are currently not attending school.

History

1986 – First Permanent Headquarters (16 tsubo), Chuo 1-5, Shiroishi-ku

2002 – Relocated Headquarters (30 tsubo), Higashi-Sapporo 6-5, Shiroishi-ku

2005 – Relocated Headquarters (36 tsubo), Chuo 1-4, Shiroishi-ku

2012 – Current Headquarters (200 tsubo)

Access

2-1-27 Sakaedori, Shiroishi-ku, Sapporo
Dedicated parking available.
Main Building: 100 tatami • Annex: 60 tatami.
About 9 minutes on foot (750m) from Shiroishi Subway Station.
About 18 minutes on foot (1.4km) from Tsukisamu-Chuo Station.
Approximately 4 minutes on foot from the Chuo Bus stop "Sakaedori 1-chome."

Parking is located behind the annex building at the rear of the property. During winter, vehicles are parked more closely together because of snow.
