Seishinkai Karate Federation - Makomanai Branch

[Minami-ku Makomanai Karate Class / Seishinkai Makomanai Branch]
The Makomanai karate class got its start in 2007 (Heisei 19) at the Makomanai Gorin Children's Hall. In 2017 (Heisei 29), with membership having declined, it merged with the Moiwa and Tokiwa branches, but it resumed independently in September 2019 (Reiwa 1).
Following the consolidation of local elementary schools, the children's hall closed, and from August 2026 (Reiwa 8) practice has been held at the Minami-ku Community Center meeting room.
We'd like to focus on Kids Karate and Junior Karate, centered on members from preschool age through junior high. We're currently looking for trial participants!
Parent-and-child participation is also more common here than at many of our other branches.
While many dojos feel like an extension of a school sports club, this one aims to offer karate for health and fitness that members can enjoy learning in a relaxed, club-like atmosphere.
※ Depending on future school consolidations, we may move again if a better venue becomes available, including a new children's hall.
