Seishinkai Karate Federation — Tokiwa Branch

Minami Ward Tokiwa Karate Class · Seishinkai Tokiwa Branch

The Tokiwa Karate Class began in September 2012. It originally operated at a local community hall before moving to the current Tokiwa Children's Hall, and has since returned to Tokiwa Danchi Community Hall, where it has been held since April 2020.
The class focuses primarily on preschool children through junior high school students, with kids and junior karate classes. Trial participants are welcome!
Parents and children are also welcome to participate together, as are students interested in a senior class.
While many dojos operate more like an extension of traditional sports clubs, our aim is to enjoy learning and practicing karate as a recreational activity focused on health and physical fitness.
