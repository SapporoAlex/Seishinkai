Seishinkai Karate-do Federation — Sakaemachi Branch

[Sakaemachi Karate Class, Seishinkai Sakaemachi Branch, Higashi Ward]
The Sakaemachi Karate Class started in September 2003 (Heisei 15), using a meeting room at San-ai Motors as its venue.
It is a karate club centered on Kids Karate, Junior Karate, and Parent-Child Karate.
We especially welcome participation from parents and children together, and from the Senior Class.
While many dojos feel like an extension of school sports clubs, we aim to make karate an enjoyable, club-like experience centered on health and fitness.

Dojo / Branch Address / Venue Training Days
★ Sakaemachi Karate Club San-ai Motors Building 3F Meeting Room, Kita 42-jo Higashi 19-chome, Higashi-ku, Sapporo Sunday 9:30-10:30Extended class 10:30-11:00

※Session 1, for beginners and elementary school students, runs 9:30-10:30; the Extended session, for intermediate level and above, runs Session 1 plus 10:30-11:00.
The venue is provided through the kind courtesy of San-ai Motors. Please ask an instructor where to park, and please do not enter areas outside the meeting room.
For inquiries, please contact the office.
