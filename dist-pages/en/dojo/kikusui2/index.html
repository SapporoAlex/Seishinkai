Seishinkai Karate Federation — Kikusui-Motomachi Branch

Kikusui-Motomachi Karate Class, Shiroishi Ward / Seishinkai Kikusui-Motomachi Branch

The Kikusui-Motomachi Karate Class began activities in April 2010 (Heisei 22) at the Kikusui-Motomachi Children's Center.
From April 2020 (Reiwa 2), the venue moved to the hall at the Kikusui-Motomachi Minami Community Hall, where members have been enjoying a good workout every Tuesday from 18:30.
Since its founding, it has run as a Junior Karate class, focused mainly on preschoolers through junior high school students.
We also welcome parent-child participants.
While many dojos feel like an extension of school sports-club activities, we aim for karate as health and fitness training — practicing and learning together in a relaxed, club-like atmosphere.
